passages across chapter roles, with particular attention to experimental
analysis. It is a qualitative review of how explanations are constructed,
not a comparison of conjunction frequencies or an endorsement of every
scientific claim in the samples.

## Riley: turn a limitation into the next experimental question

The transition to subsampling on p. 31 first identifies what the preceding
experiments have assumed: observations directly generated by the network.
It then explains how the recording process changes those observations and
uses that limitation to motivate a more realistic test. On p. 33, the
reconstruction discussion proceeds from the observed effect to the propagator
being estimated and then to the effect on inferred connection strengths.
The same objects carry the explanation across sentences.

The aggregation paragraph on p. 46 provides an especially clear chain:
the available observations make individual reconstruction unreliable;
aggregation is therefore tested; averaging and concatenation are compared;
and their different outcomes are related to noise and model assumptions.
The short sentence “We therefore evaluated aggregation strategies” has a
specific job: it makes the earlier limitation the reason for the next action.
The phrase “consistent with” also signals that an interpretation is being
related to a model rather than introduced as another plotted observation.

The transfer to our thesis is the question-to-check transition and the stable
comparison order. It is not a reason to add generic roadmaps, reproduce every
grammatical construction or treat a qualitative diagnostic as causal proof.

## Grace: connect displayed evidence, interpretation and method choice

The dynamic-connectivity discussion on p. 40 introduces a visible matrix
feature, explains the duplicated orientation through symmetry, interprets
the low correlations and relates the relevant dates to known events. The
successive subjects remain tied to the displayed feature. Cause, mathematical
property and interpretation have different roles, and the prose signals
those roles instead of merely listing features.

The method comparison on p. 48 starts from the preceding analysis, identifies
the fixed-window limitation and motivates an alternative without that
restriction. The method paragraph then links the chosen number of states to
comparability and the matching procedure to variation between runs. The
connectors are useful because the experimental reasons are stated explicitly.

The stock-level and sector-level discussions on p. 35 use different paragraphs
for different comparison levels. The limitations on p. 62 similarly keep an
exchange-coverage qualification, its explanation, an example and its effect
on generalisability together. Our corresponding lesson is to finish a
comparison at one level before switching to another, and to attach a
qualification to the claim whose scope it restricts.

Some introductory and concluding passages use generic announcements or
repeated additive transitions. Those are not requirements to transfer.

## Michael: put conditions before trends and carry the result into its mechanism

The synthetic-results discussion on p. 35 places increasing spike count
before the changes in estimates and uncertainty. Its comparison between
neighbourhood sizes then carries the performance difference into a variance
explanation, including a qualification about bias. A separate paragraph
introduces the effect of embedding dimension, so the reader can identify
which experimental parameter is changing.

The refractory-process analysis on p. 37 separates varying the refractory
period from varying spike count. Within each comparison, the setting precedes
the finding, and the account progresses from early error to convergence.
The method derivation on p. 25 also explains what a substitution achieves
before proceeding to the next transformation. These passages demonstrate
that flow can come from information order and named quantities even when
individual mathematical steps remain separate sentences.

Some numerical conclusions use language stronger than finite experiments
alone establish. Our revision should preserve the narrower qualifications
supported by our own mathematics and diagnostics, and retain Australian
English rather than copying the samples' regional spelling inconsistencies.

## Operational lessons for the Welch MI thesis

1. Identify the question a paragraph answers before joining its sentences.
2. Put the condition or experimental choice before the result that depends
   on it, then carry a named estimate or error into the explanation.
3. Use a conjunction only after identifying the relationship: supported
   cause, consequence, qualification, comparison or changing condition.
4. Keep comparison axes stable. Finish small-sample behaviour before recovery,
   and distinguish method differences from the explanation of a curve's shape.
5. Place a reminder where it qualifies a definition or finding. A detached
   reminder often needs moving rather than another connector.
6. Preserve useful definitions and mathematical steps. Clear exposition does
   not require all sentences or paragraphs to become longer.
7. Review the complete rewritten paragraph for meaning, rhythm and evidence
   strength, including whether a new because or therefore overstates a cause.
