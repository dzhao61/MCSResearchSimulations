## If More Methodological Novelty Is Required

Develop one coherent extension: simultaneous inference over an MI-difference
matrix.

The extension would:

- estimate the vector of pairwise MI differences;
- estimate cross-pair covariance from joint influence functions;
- control family-wise error or false discovery rate;
- compare marginal testing, max-statistic Gaussian approximation, and
  studentized permutation; and
- target the same applied MI-network use case that motivated the thesis.

This is preferable to reviving the rejected saddlepoint because it solves a
real multiple-comparison problem in the motivating applications.
