\chapter{Complete Scenario Specifications}
\label{app:scenario-specifications}

Table~\ref{tab:appendix-scenario-map} records the realised sample sizes and
minimum true joint expected counts for every fixed population pair in the
primary calibration experiment. The population tables themselves contain
many cell probabilities and are retained without rounding in
\texttt{population\_scenarios.csv}. That file is the authoritative
machine-readable specification.

In the table, $E_{\min}^{P}=\min_{i,j}n_Pp_{ij}$ and
$E_{\min}^{Q}=\min_{i,j}n_Qq_{ij}$. Every row represents 10,000 independently
sampled table pairs under the exact equal-MI null. The largest numerical
departure from $I(P)=I(Q)$ was $8.5\times10^{-14}$ nats.
